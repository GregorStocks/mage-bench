- **Model-only**: Mistral Medium briefly sent three invalid `mana_plan` strings (`MstMed Rivals_pilot.log:95-105`) before recovering with a correct spell cast. Not a platform bug.
- **No code/runtime bug in this specimen**: `errors.log` is clean, end-of-game bridge shutdown warnings are expected, and the candidate \"UI/state\" bugs were all contradicted by raw logs.
