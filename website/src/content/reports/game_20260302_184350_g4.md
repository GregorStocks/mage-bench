## The Matchup

GptOSS Snark (GPT-OSS 120B) faces Haiku Sport (Claude Haiku 4.5) in a Limited duel. What should be a straightforward game of Jumpstart creatures trading blows becomes a one-sided affair thanks to a catastrophic mulligan decision.

## The Hand That Launched a Thousand Losses

The game's defining moment happens before a single land is played. GptOSS Snark looks at an opening hand of four Forests and three Mountains — [seven lands, zero spells](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=0) — and keeps it. This is perhaps the clearest mulligan in Magic: a hand with literally no ability to affect the board. Even going down to four cards with a single spell would be a massive improvement. GPT-OSS doesn't just stumble here; it walks confidently off a cliff.

Meanwhile, Haiku Sport keeps a functional hand with Feral Prowler, Canopy Stalker, Scale the Heights, and three Forests — a reasonable curve with early plays and card draw upside.

## Haiku Sport Builds Unopposed

With its opponent drawing lands and passing, Haiku Sport gets to execute its gameplan without any resistance. [Feral Prowler arrives on turn 3](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=28), followed by [Pouncing Cheetah on turn 5](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=59) — a flash creature with 3 power that starts applying real pressure.

GptOSS Snark finally casts a spell on turn 4: Dragon Fodder, producing two 1/1 Goblin tokens. But when Haiku Sport attacks on turn 7, the Goblins trade down — one dies to Feral Prowler, the other to Pouncing Cheetah — and [GptOSS Snark is left with an empty board](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=89) while Haiku Sport still has two creatures standing.

## The Bleeding Continues

[Turn 9](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=114) brings Canopy Stalker, a 4/2 that joins the assault. GptOSS Snark drops to 14 life. Then [Benevolent Hydra](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=138) arrives on turn 11, and a three-creature attack on turn 12 smashes GptOSS Snark down to 5 life.

To make matters worse, GptOSS Snark times out twice at critical moments. On turn 10, it fails to even play a land despite holding five of them — missing the mana needed for Hunter's Edge, which could have removed a key attacker. On the final turn at 5 life, [it times out again](/games/game_20260302_184350_g4?s=150), failing to cast Orazca Frillback as a desperately needed blocker. Domesticated Hydra joins Haiku Sport's army, and four creatures crash in for lethal.

## Key Decisions

**The seven-land keep** — The single biggest factor in this game. GptOSS Snark's reasoning shows confusion throughout the early game, with repeated attempts to pass priority and requests for game state that suggest the model was struggling with basic game mechanics. Keeping a hand with zero spells is indefensible at any level of play.

**Haiku Sport's clean sequencing** — In contrast, Haiku Sport's reasoning is crisp and purposeful:

> With only one land drop available this turn, I'll play a Forest to establish my mana base. I don't have enough mana to cast Feral Prowler yet (needs {1}{G} = 2 mana).

Simple, correct, efficient. It curves out Feral Prowler into Pouncing Cheetah into Canopy Stalker into Benevolent Hydra — textbook Limited aggression.

**The timeout cascade** — GptOSS Snark's reasoning samples reveal a model that's confused about game state, repeatedly trying to query and re-query state rather than making decisions. This manifests as multiple timeouts at moments where any action would be better than none.

## Verdict

Haiku Sport wins convincingly at 22 life to 0, never taking more than a single point of damage the entire game. The result was sealed before the first turn — a seven-land keep is an unrecoverable deficit against any competent opponent. GptOSS Snark compounded the problem with timeout-induced missed plays at critical junctures. Haiku Sport didn't need to do anything fancy; it just played creatures on curve and attacked, which is exactly what you should do when your opponent gives you 13 turns of free development.
