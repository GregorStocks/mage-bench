# game_20260227_004918

**Players**: Gem25P Lucky (google/gemini-2.5-pro, Doomsday) vs GLM47 Gramps (z-ai/glm-4.7, Eldrazi Stompy)
**Format**: Legacy (Two Player Duel)
**Result**: GLM47 Gramps won on turn 15
**Config**: round-robin-1v1

## Issues Filed

- **P1** `doomsday-pile-target-id-mismatch`: Doomsday pile ordering fails — GAME_TARGET IDs rejected despite being listed in choices. Short ID→UUID mappings shift between choice presentation and LLM response, causing 9 consecutive failures. The bridge auto-selected random cards, making Doomsday unplayable. Root cause: BridgeCallbackHandler.java:1567-1570 compares resolved UUIDs against cached lastChoices UUIDs, but ShortIdRegistry.register() can remap IDs between calls.

- **P2** `mana-plan-unknown-short-id-crash`: GAME_PLAY_MANA crashes on remapped short ID (p30), causing 10-minute loop on turn 5. Gem25P tried to cast Consider 15+ times with mana_plan=["p30"] (Lotus Petal), but p30 was no longer in the registry. IllegalArgumentException at ShortIdRegistry.java:69 is uncaught, causing the bridge to re-present the same prompt. Root cause: handleGamePlayManaAuto at BridgeCallbackHandler.java:5057 doesn't catch IllegalArgumentException.

## Findings

### Game Narrative

Gramps deployed Turn 2 Thought-Knot Seer (double Eldrazi Temple), exiling Lucky's Daze. Turn 5 Chalice of the Void on X=1 locked out nearly all of Lucky's 1-mana spells (Ponder, Brainstorm, Consider, Dark Ritual, Daze). Reality Smasher on Turn 7. Second Thought-Knot Seer on Turn 9 exiled Force of Will. Lucky was locked down for turns 6-13.

Turn 5 had a 10-minute Consider loop (Bug #2 above). Lucky tried to cast Consider with Lotus Petal 15+ times, each failing with "Unknown short ID: p30". Loop detector eventually broke the cycle.

Turn 14: Lucky at 4 life cast Doomsday (going to 2), but couldn't construct the pile (Bug #1 above). 9 of 10 ordering attempts failed. Bridge auto-selected a random pile. Turn 15: Gramps cast Eldrazi Linebreaker, gave Reality Smasher +4/+0, swung for lethal 9.

### Model Behavior (Not Filed)

- **Gem25P Lucky (Gemini 2.5 Pro)**: Strong strategic reasoning — correctly identified Brainstorm lock self-diagnosis, evaluated Doomsday pile theory. Lost due to platform bugs (unplayable Doomsday pile, Consider loop), not strategic failures. 12 failed tool calls, 2 empty LLM responses.
- **GLM47 Gramps (GLM-4.7)**: Missed lethal attacks on turns 11 and 13 (tried to declare attackers during GAME_SELECT phase instead of waiting for declare_attackers). Had Thought-Knot Seers + Reality Smasher (13+ power) against Lucky at 5 life. Won eventually on turn 15 despite these misses. 5 failed tool calls, wrong parameter types for GAME_CHOOSE_ABILITY, post-game chat failure ("no chat session").

### Other Observations

- **Lost callback recovery**: Numerous 10-second warnings during the Consider loop and Doomsday pile construction. The bridge waited 185+ seconds for callbacks. Recovery mechanism (speculative passes) worked but is noisy.
- **Empty LLM responses**: 4 total (2 each player). The one at the Doomsday selection→ordering transition (01:16:09) may have contributed to the pile ordering failure.
- **send_chat_message failure**: GLM47 tried to send post-game chat 16 seconds after game end. Chat session was already cleaned up. Third occurrence of this pattern across analyzed games.
- **Both out-of-range diagnostics classified as model misuse**: Gem25P tried index=1 when only 0 existed (Polluted Delta). Gramps tried index=1 without loading choices. No suspicious races detected.

## Notes

- Both P1 and P2 stem from the same fundamental issue: ShortIdRegistry mappings are mutable and can change between when the LLM sees choices and when it responds. P1 is harder to fix (needs architectural change to lastChoices storage). P2 has a straightforward defensive fix (try-catch in handleGamePlayManaAuto).
- Doomsday is likely broken for all models. Any model playing the Doomsday deck will hit the pile ordering bug. This should be prioritized since it makes a key Legacy combo deck unplayable.
- The attack-out-of-phase pattern (GLM47 sending attackers during GAME_SELECT) has been seen in other games across multiple models. Models don't properly track when they're in the declare_attackers step vs playing spells.
