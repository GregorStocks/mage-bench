## Notes

- When annotations and decisions disagree, the decision stack and `subsequentActions` are more trustworthy than the blunder text. This game is a clean example: the false major blunder disappears once you inspect the later combat-stack records.
- `mcp_errors.py` was enough to clear the three Opus tool failures as model-only. The important follow-up work here came from targeted `jq` over `decisions` and `annotations`, not from deeper error analysis.
