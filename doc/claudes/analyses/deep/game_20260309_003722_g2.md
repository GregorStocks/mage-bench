**Result**: GPT54 Sport won on turn 13
**Config**: seed7-vs-seed3

## Findings

- Filed: `export-decisions-drop-successful-choose-action-retries` (P3) — the export/analysis pipeline recorded Gem3P's failed `choice="Black"` retry for Thriving Heath, dropped the later successful `text="Black"` retry into a blank follow-up decision, and generated a false timeout/default-color blunder annotation.
- Filed: `game-choose-choice-choice-param-returns-short-id-error` (P3) — `GAME_CHOOSE_CHOICE` still routes `choice="Black"` through short-ID resolution and returns `Unknown short ID: Black` instead of a structured `invalid_choice` telling the model to use `text=Name` or `choice=N`.
- No crash or stall bug affected gameplay in this match. The remaining annotations are ordinary model-quality blunders rather than platform failures.

## Notes

- This game is a good reminder not to trust a single blunder annotation around `GAME_CHOOSE_CHOICE` without checking raw `*_llm.jsonl`. The export said Gem3P timed out on the color pick, but the raw log showed a successful retry 16 seconds later.
- Chat/personality was contained to chat messages here. I did not find personality infection in the gameplay traces; the main technical problems were the bridge retry UX and the exporter mis-recording the retry sequence.
