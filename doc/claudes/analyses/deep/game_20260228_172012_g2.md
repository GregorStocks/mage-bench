# game_20260228_172012_g2

**Players**: Opus Teach (claude-opus-4.6) vs GPT52 Philo (gpt-5.2)
**Format**: Limited (Jumpstart), Two Player Duel
**Result**: Opus Teach won on turn 17
**Config**: round-robin

## Findings

- **P1 filed**: `client-side-yield-cancels-pending-target` — Bridge unconditionally cancels pending GAME_TARGET when pass_priority/get_action_choices called with client-side yield (until=stack_resolved). Root cause at BridgeCallbackHandler.java:3043-3046. GPT52 Philo tried to cast Flames of the Firebrand 9 times and activate Chandra 8 times, all cancelled by this bug.
- **P3 filed**: `gpt52-until-param-abuse-on-get-action-choices` — GPT-5.2 adds `until` param to 100% of get_action_choices calls. Model-level toolset mismatch.
- **Not filed (model behavior)**: GPT52 Philo had 80 timeout annotations, but these were actually the model stuck in pass_priority/get_action_choices loops, not real timeouts. The loop detector fired 5 times.
- **Not filed (minor/known)**: Opus Teach's get_game_state showed Ironroot Warlord as playable=true but get_action_choices didn't include it (auto-tap couldn't handle multicolor with non-standard mana sources). Model worked around it with explicit mana_plan next turn.
- **Not filed (benign)**: 8 FileNotFoundException for missing sound files in spectator (headless env). 25 out-of-range diagnostics all classified as model misuse.

## Notes

- GPT-5.2 made 268 tool calls vs Opus Teach's 121. 2639s thinking time vs 592s. The entire excess is from stuck loops.
- Chandra, Heart of Fire was on the board for 19 minutes without a single successful activation.
- The Outnumber self-kill on turn 4 (killing own Goblin Arsonist to trigger death ability) was actually a clever play, not a misplay.
- The P1 bridge bug affects any model that calls pass_priority/get_action_choices with client-side yields during a pending non-priority action. Opus doesn't hit it because it never uses `until` on get_action_choices and uses pass_priority(until=...) only when no targeting is pending.
