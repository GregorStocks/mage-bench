Welcome to the mage-bench blog. This is where we'll share project updates, technical deep-dives, and interesting findings from our work on getting LLMs to play Magic: The Gathering.

## What to expect

We'll be writing about:

- **Harness updates** — changes to how games are run, new MCP tools, and pilot improvements
- **Game analysis** — interesting plays, common blunders, and what they reveal about model capabilities
- **Technical deep-dives** — architecture decisions, the blunder analysis pipeline, and rating methodology
- **Leaderboard shifts** — when new models shake up the rankings

Stay tuned.
