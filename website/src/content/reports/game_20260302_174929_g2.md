## The Matchup

GLM47 Pessim (GLM-4.7) pilots a UR tempo deck with goblin tokens, bounce spells, and burn. Gem31P Proud (Gemini 3.1 Pro) has a WB deck anchored by the legendary Taranika, Akroan Veteran. The cost disparity is notable — Gem31P spends $0.78 with 2,089 seconds of thinking time to GLM47's $0.30 and 607 seconds. All that extra thinking doesn't help.

## Early Game

GLM47 curves out perfectly: Mountain on turn 2, Island on turn 4, [Thriving Isle](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=38) on turn 6. Meanwhile Gem31P is stuck on a single Plains for five consecutive turns, accumulating a hand of 8 cards by turn 6 with no way to cast anything meaningful.

On [turn 8](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=58), GLM47 fires off [Hordeling Outburst](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=58) — three 1/1 Goblin tokens flood the board. Gem31P's response on turn 9 is a Plaguecrafter with no creatures of its own to sacrifice, forcing a self-discard while only trading for a single Goblin token. A terrible exchange.

## Mid Game

GLM47 follows with [Rummaging Goblin](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=95) for card filtering and starts attacking. Two Goblin tokens swing for 2 on [turn 10](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=95), dropping Gem31P to 18. Then comes the knockout turn.

On [turn 12](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=128), GLM47 attacks with three creatures and casts [Burn Bright](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=128), pumping all attackers +2/+0. The Goblins and Rummaging Goblin slam for 9 damage, cratering Gem31P to 9 life. The annotations note GLM47 could have used Flame Lash to remove Taranika instead — but with the opponent at 9, raw aggression was defensible.

Gem31P finally deploys [Taranika, Akroan Veteran](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=107) on turn 11 alongside Angelic Protector on turn 13. Taranika attacks and triggers, buffing Angelic Protector. But GLM47 has the answers: [Chilling Trap](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=160) taps Taranika down mid-combat, then [Crashing Tide](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=189) bounces the legend back to hand entirely.

## Late Game

Gem31P replays Taranika on [turn 15](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=207) alongside Durable Coilbug, building a last-stand board. But GLM47 has assembled an overwhelming force: two Goblins, Rummaging Goblin, and Universal Automaton all attack. Gem31P blocks but can't handle everything, and [Flame Lash](/games/game_20260302_174929_g2?s=207) to the face delivers the final 4 damage for the kill.

## Key Decisions

**GLM47's Hordeling Outburst sequencing**: The annotations flag that casting Bloodline Pretender first (choosing Goblin) would have let it grow with each subsequent Goblin. A moderate missed synergy, but the raw token pressure was enough regardless.

**Gem31P's Plaguecrafter with no board**: Casting a sacrifice-each-player effect when you have zero creatures means you're discarding a card while the opponent only loses a token. Gem31P should have held it until it had creatures to protect.

**GLM47's bounce tempo**: Chilling Trap into Crashing Tide on Taranika was a clean two-card answer that bought two full turns of unopposed attacks. The tempo advantage was insurmountable.

## Verdict

GLM47 Pessim wins 20-0, never taking a point of damage — a perfect life total through 15 turns. The game was decided by Gem31P's mana drought (one Plains until turn 9) combined with GLM47's efficient aggro curve. GLM47 made some suboptimal sequencing choices but executed the core aggro gameplan flawlessly: deploy threats, remove blockers, burn to finish. Gem31P's extra thinking time ($0.78 vs $0.30) went entirely to waste, spent reasoning about cards it couldn't cast.
