## The Matchup

GPT-5.2 brings a green-black deck with an all-star lineup: Thrashing Brontodon (a sturdy 3/4), Gorging Vulture, Suspicious Shambler, and the headliner — Ghalta, Primal Hunger, a 12/12 trampler whose cost drops by 1 for each point of power among your creatures. Llama 4 Maverick pilots a red-white deck with Rigging Runner, Celestial Enforcer, and combat tricks including Moment of Heroism and Aerial Assault. On paper, the combat tricks could give Llama 4 the edge in creature exchanges. In practice, the model barely engages with the game.

## The Rigging Runner Experiment

Llama 4 Snark opens with [Rigging Runner on turn 4](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=35), a 1/1 first striker. Without the raid trigger (no creature attacked this turn), it stays small. GPT-5.2 answers with [Thrashing Brontodon](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=48) — a 3/4 body that immediately dominates the board.

The Runner pings in for 1 damage on [turn 6](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=64), though GPT-5.2 should have blocked with its Brontodon for a free kill. On [turn 8](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=106), Llama 4 casts Moment of Heroism on the unblocked Runner for 3 extra damage and 3 life, pushing its total to 20 while GPT-5.2 drops to 17. It's the right idea — using a trick on an unblocked creature for a life swing — but spending the card for just incremental value when it could save a creature in combat later is questionable.

## GPT-5.2 Builds the Machine

Meanwhile, GPT-5.2 steadily widens its board. [Gorging Vulture on turn 7](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=84) mills cards and provides an aerial body. [Suspicious Shambler on turn 9](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=126) adds a hefty 4/2. With Thrashing Brontodon still anchoring the ground, GPT-5.2's power on board climbs toward critical mass.

[Turn 10](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=149) brings the pivotal attack: all three creatures swing in — Brontodon (3), Vulture (2), and Shambler (4) for 9 total damage. Llama 4 Snark has Celestial Enforcer (a 2/3) on defense and declines to block anything, dropping from 15 to 6 life. The Enforcer could have killed the Gorging Vulture while surviving, or traded with the Suspicious Shambler, saving at least 4 life. Taking 9 uncontested is a major error at a critical life total.

## Enter Ghalta

[Turn 11](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=175) is where the game ends in spirit. GPT-5.2 has 9 power on board, reducing Ghalta's cost from {10}{G}{G} to just {1}{G}{G} — three mana for a 12/12 trampler. GPT-5.2 instead casts Pit Keeper (a 2/1 that does nothing with only one creature in the graveyard), delaying Ghalta by a turn. But it hardly matters — [Ghalta arrives on turn 12](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=175) and the game is functionally over.

Llama 4 Snark's last gasp comes on [turn 12](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=200): it casts Keldon Raider and attacks with Celestial Enforcer directly into the summoning-sick Ghalta, which blocks and devours it. With the Enforcer gone, there's nothing to even slow down the dinosaur. [Ghalta attacks on turn 13](/games/game_20260302_005956_g3?s=200), Keldon Raider steps in front and dies, but 12 power tramples over a 4-toughness blocker for 8 damage — more than enough against 6 life.

## Key Decisions

**No blocks on the 9-damage swing (Turn 10)** — Llama 4 Snark takes a full 9 damage with a 2/3 available to block. Blocking Gorging Vulture saves 2 life and kills a creature; blocking Suspicious Shambler trades the Enforcer but saves 4 life. Either preserves a chance to survive Ghalta's arrival. Taking 9 and dropping to 6 seals the game.

**Ghalta delayed by Pit Keeper (Turn 11)** — GPT-5.2 had the mana and power discount to slam Ghalta a turn early, which would have been immediately lethal with trample. Casting Pit Keeper first was suboptimal but didn't change the outcome.

**Llama 4's reasoning quality** — The model's reasoning traces reveal almost no strategic engagement:

> I'll continue by calling `pass_priority()`. The output from this function will guide my next decision.

This is autopilot — the model passes priority mechanically without analyzing the board state or planning ahead. The contrast with its opponent's clean curve-out is stark.

## Verdict

GPT-5.2 wins 17-6 in a game defined by efficient creature deployment capped by Magic's most iconic cost-reduction payoff. Ghalta turning 12 mana into 3 is exactly the kind of synergy that wins Limited games, and GPT-5.2 assembled the pieces methodically. Llama 4 Maverick's shallow reasoning and failure to engage with blocking decisions left it unable to mount any resistance once the dinosaur appeared. The combat trick that brought it to 20 life on turn 8 was a distant memory by turn 13.
