## The Matchup

Grok4F Valley (Grok 4 Fast) runs a UW deck with Basri Ket, Basri's Acolyte, Coastal Piracy, and Spectral Sailor — a card advantage engine that should bury most opponents. MiniMx Proud (MiniMax M2.5) brings a BR aggro deck with Archfiend's Vessel, Ashmouth Hound, Crypt Lurker, and Seismic Elemental. Neither model exposes reasoning traces, so this report relies purely on the plays.

## Early Game

MiniMx opens with [Archfiend's Vessel](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=20) on turn 3 — a 1/1 with a powerful death-and-return trigger that could create a 5/5 Demon. Grok4F immediately [bounces it](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=33) with Voyage's End, resetting the threat. MiniMx replays the Vessel and adds [Ashmouth Hound](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=75), a 2/1 that pings blockers.

Grok4F deploys the cornerstone [Basri's Acolyte](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=91) on turn 8 — a 2/3 lifelink that distributes +1/+1 counters on entry. The Acolyte blocks the Hound, surviving the 1-damage ping trigger and trading blows.

## Mid Game

MiniMx makes its biggest mistake on [turn 9](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=115): using [Eliminate](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=115) to save Ashmouth Hound during combat when both creatures were already going to trade. Premium removal spent to save a 2/1 — brutal.

Grok4F lands [Basri Ket](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=136) on turn 10, the planeswalker that keeps pumping Basri's Acolyte with +1/+1 counters and indestructible. The Acolyte grows from 2/3 to 3/4 to 4/5, lifelinking Grok4F back up each attack. By turn 12, Grok4F adds [Coastal Piracy](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=179) — now every combat damage trigger draws a card. The engine is online.

MiniMx compounds errors: on [turn 9](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=115), it uses Reanimate (paying 2 life) to bring back the lowly 2/1 Ashmouth Hound instead of leveraging the 5 mana to cast Archfiend's Vessel then Return from the graveyard, which would create a 5/5 Demon. Then on turn 11, it passes the postcombat main with 4 untapped lands and three castable spells while Grok4F just landed a planeswalker.

## Late Game

Despite building an overwhelming engine, Grok4F can't close the game. Life totals yo-yo: Grok4F heals to 22, then 26, then 27 through lifelink, but MiniMx keeps swinging with Crypt Lurker (3/4) and whatever else survives. [Seismic Elemental](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=256) on turn 15 forces ground creatures unable to block, crashing through for 4 damage.

The critical problem is that Grok4F draws too many cards and never deploys them. By [turn 18](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=332), Grok4F has 7 cards in hand and 26 life but only Basri's Acolyte and Spectral Sailor on board. Meanwhile MiniMx keeps pressing with direct damage — [Magma Jet](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=306) to the face, Ashmouth Hound pings, Crypt Lurker attacks.

On [turn 19](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=344), MiniMx swings with Crypt Lurker and Archfiend's Vessel. Grok4F, now at 22 life, can't stabilize — the repeated attacks through multiple turns have added up, and MiniMx lands the killing blow.

## Key Decisions

**MiniMx's Eliminate waste**: Using premium removal to save a 2/1 from a trade that was already happening was the game's most clear-cut mistake. That Eliminate could have answered Basri Ket or a future bomb.

**MiniMx's Reanimate on Ashmouth Hound**: Paying 2 life to get back a 2/1 when a Demon-creating line existed was a major missed opportunity. The 5/5 flying Demon would have dramatically changed the race math.

**Magma Jet face vs. Basri Ket**: On [turn 17](/games/game_20260302_005956_g4?s=306), MiniMx aimed Magma Jet at Grok4F's face (27 to 25 life) instead of Basri Ket (loyalty 5 to 3). Killing the planeswalker would have shut down the +1/+1 counter engine, but dealing face damage kept the clock ticking — a debatable but ultimately vindicated choice.

## Verdict

MiniMx Proud wins despite making several major tactical errors, proving that consistent aggression can overcome a superior card advantage engine when the engine player doesn't deploy enough blockers. Grok4F Valley had Basri Ket pumping counters, Coastal Piracy drawing cards, and Spectral Sailor as an instant-speed draw outlet — yet still lost because it accumulated cards in hand rather than converting them to board presence. MiniMx played a sloppy but functional beatdown strategy, and sometimes that's enough.
