## The Matchup

GLM-4.7 pilots a blue-black snow deck with serious synergy: Marit Lage's Slumber, Avalanche Caller (which animates snow lands into 4/4 hexproof creatures), and the legendary Isu the Abominable. Mistral Medium 3.1 has a green-black deck packed with solid creatures — Ravenous Chupacabra, Canopy Stalker, Slate Street Ruffian — and disruption in Mind Rot. But the game is decided before it really begins, thanks to a disastrous mulligan decision.

## A Mulligan Gone Wrong

MstMed Pessim's opening hand has five lands and one castable spell. Reasonable to mulligan. The six-card hand is much better — Feral Prowler, Slate Street Ruffian, Canopy Stalker, Ravenous Chupacabra, Mind Rot, and two lands. The problem comes at the bottom step: MstMed Pessim [bottoms a Forest](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=0), keeping just one land in a hand where four of six cards need black mana. As the model's own reasoning put it:

> I'll bottom the second Forest since I only need one to start, and the rest of the hand is already well-balanced.

This is catastrophic. With only one Forest and no Swamps, every black card is uncastable. MstMed Pessim proceeds to sit on a single Forest for the first seven turns of the game, never drawing a second land until turn 7 and never finding a Swamp at all.

## GLM47 Builds Its Snow Engine

While its opponent draws and discards, GLM47 Stoic methodically assembles its snow engine. [Marit Lage's Slumber arrives on turn 4](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=39) — an enchantment that scries whenever a snow permanent enters, building toward a legendary 20/20 token. [Avalanche Caller on turn 6](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=63) provides both a body and the ability to animate snow lands into 4/4 hexproof hasty elementals.

GLM47 makes some sequencing errors along the way. On turn 8, it casts Spark Reaper before playing a land, missing a free scry from Marit Lage's Slumber. On turn 10, it deploys [Suspicious Shambler (a 4/2)](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=126) when it could have played a Swamp first and cast [Isu the Abominable](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=179) — a 5/5 legendary snow creature that grows whenever snow spells are cast. Isu doesn't arrive until turn 12, two turns later than necessary.

## MstMed's Lone Stand

MstMed Pessim's only meaningful play comes on [turn 9](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=102) with Feral Prowler — a 1/3 cat that draws a card when it dies. On turn 11, it tries to use the Prowler aggressively, casting Nature's Way to have it fight Spark Reaper. But Feral Prowler has only 1 power against a 3-toughness creature — the Reaper survives easily and the spell accomplishes nothing.

Feral Prowler does manage to trade with the Suspicious Shambler on [turn 13](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=186), drawing a card on death, but by then GLM47 has Isu growing on board and the game is hopelessly out of reach.

## The Three Missed Lethals

The most remarkable aspect of this game is that GLM47 Stoic misses lethal damage on three consecutive turns — and still wins comfortably.

[Turn 15](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=199): MstMed Pessim is at 9 life with no creatures. GLM47 has Avalanche Caller and enough mana to animate two snow lands into 4/4 hexproof haste creatures. Two 4/4s plus Spark Reaper (2 power) plus Avalanche Caller (1 power) equals 11 damage — lethal. Instead, GLM47 only animates one land and attacks for less.

[Turn 16](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=227): Same board state, same math, same mistake. The opponent is still at 9 with no blockers. GLM47 again fails to activate Avalanche Caller a second time, wasting Spark Reaper's sacrifice ability to draw cards instead of just attacking for the win.

[Turn 17](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=259): At 9 life facing Isu (now a 6/6) and Avalanche Caller, a single land animation plus Isu equals 10 damage — still lethal. This time GLM47 does attack, bringing MstMed Pessim to 1 life but still not finishing the job.

GLM47 finally closes it out on [turn 18](/games/game_20260302_184350_g1?s=266), deploying Moodmark Painter to give Isu menace and a power boost for an unblockable lethal swing. As the model's reasoning noted:

> The opponent is at 1 life! I need to finish them. I'll cast Moodmark Painter to give Isu menace and +2/+0, making it a 9/7 that can't be blocked.

An elaborate solution to a problem that should have been solved three turns earlier.

## Key Decisions

**The fatal bottom (Mulligan)** — Bottoming the second Forest left MstMed Pessim with a one-land hand in a two-color deck that needs black mana for most of its spells. This single decision cost the entire game.

**Nature's Way on Spark Reaper (Turn 11)** — Using a 1-power creature to fight a 3-toughness creature accomplishes nothing. The spell was simply wasted.

**Three missed lethals (Turns 15-17)** — GLM47's failure to recognize that two Avalanche Caller activations produced lethal damage extended the game by three turns. The model understood individual pieces (animating lands, attacking) but couldn't combine them into a lethal calculation.

## Verdict

GLM47 Stoic wins 22-0 in a game that should have ended on turn 15. The snow synergy engine is powerful and GLM47 understood how to assemble it, but the model's inability to count to lethal — missing the same 11-damage line three turns in a row — reveals a significant gap in combat math. MstMed Pessim never had a chance after the mulligan, spending the entire game stuck on one or two green sources with a hand full of uncastable black spells. Sometimes the mulligan is the whole game.
