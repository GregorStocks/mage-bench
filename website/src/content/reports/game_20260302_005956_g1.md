## The Matchup

A Jumpstart duel between Llama 4 Maverick and MiniMax M2.5 (medium reasoning effort). Llama4 Brewer opened a black-focused pool with zombie synergies and recursion. MiniMx Johnny drew into a red-white deck with Basri Ket and burn potential. On paper, both sides had the tools for a competitive game. In practice, only one side would ever cast a spell.

## The Mulligan Ordeal

Before the game even began, Llama4 stumbled into a mulligan loop — asked repeatedly whether to mulligan down to 6, it kept trying `choose_action(choice="no")` and getting re-prompted. After eight attempts it finally broke free, keeping a serviceable hand of four Swamps, Blighted Bat, Liliana's Devotee, and Phyrexian Reclamation. Not the smoothest start, but the hand itself was solid.

MiniMx Johnny, meanwhile, kept its opening seven without incident.

## Early Game (Turns 1-6)

Llama4 Brewer played a textbook opening: [Swamp on turn 1](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=0), Thriving Moor choosing white on turn 3, then Phyrexian Reclamation for long-game recursion insurance. By turn 5 it deployed [Blighted Bat](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=34) — a 2/1 flyer with optional haste — giving it the first creature on board.

Across the table, MiniMx Johnny did almost nothing. It played a Mountain on turn 2 and a Thriving Heath on turn 4, choosing red. But through turns 2-6, it repeatedly passed priority without casting a single spell despite holding a full hand of seven cards. The blunder analysis flagged this as a **major** error — with no creatures and no board presence, Johnny was hemorrhaging tempo against an opponent steadily building a board.

## Mid Game (Turns 7-10)

Turn 7 was where Llama4 seized total control. It cast [Liliana's Devotee](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=63) — a 3/2 that creates zombie tokens when creatures die — and swung with Blighted Bat for 3 unblocked damage. Johnny had no creatures to block with. Life totals: 20-17.

On [turn 8](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=81), Llama4 made a notable misplay: it cast Liliana's Standard Bearer, a creature whose power is a death-trigger card draw ability. But nothing had died that turn, so the ETB was completely wasted. Still, with no opposition, even suboptimal plays built an insurmountable board.

By [turn 10](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=125), Llama4 had equipped Malefic Scythe onto Liliana's Standard Bearer (making it a 5/2) and attacked for 8 damage with the equipped Bearer and Blighted Bat. Johnny dropped to 14, then to 6 after the turn 12 attack. The choice to equip the ground creature instead of the flyer was flagged as a minor blunder — putting Malefic Scythe on Blighted Bat would have created a 5/1 evasive threat — but with zero blockers on the other side, it hardly mattered.

## The Collapse

MiniMx Johnny's turns 10-13 were a cascade of desperate, ineffective plays. It attempted to [cast Basri Ket](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=132) (costing 1WW) with only one white source, forcing a rollback. It then cast Basri's Solidarity with zero creatures on the battlefield — a spell that buffs creatures, doing literally nothing. It discarded Chandra's Pyreling (a cheap potential blocker) instead of the expensive Double Vision.

At 6 life facing a 3/1 flyer it couldn't block, Johnny cast Chandra's Pyreling and Thrill of Possibility instead of deploying Concordia Pegasus — a 1/3 flyer that could actually have blocked Blighted Bat. On [turn 13](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=178), Llama4 Brewer cast Sheoldred, Whispering One (a complete overkill at this point) and attacked for the kill.

## Key Decisions

**MiniMx Johnny's paralysis (Turns 2-6)**: The defining failure of the game. With a full hand including castable creatures, Johnny passed priority turn after turn with no plays. This wasn't a mana issue — it had Mountains in play. The blunder analysis rated this a major error, and it's hard to disagree: five turns of doing nothing against any opponent is fatal.

**Llama4's wasted Standard Bearer (Turn 8)**: [Casting Liliana's Standard Bearer](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=81) with nothing dying that turn threw away its card-draw trigger. A moderate blunder, though the board advantage was already overwhelming.

**Johnny's Basri's Solidarity on empty board (Turn 12)**: [Casting a creature-buff spell](/games/game_20260302_005956_g1?s=153) with zero creatures is about as bad as it gets — a complete waste of mana and a card at a moment when every resource was precious.

## Verdict

Llama4 Brewer won this game 20-0 in 13 turns, never taking a single point of damage. But the real story is MiniMx Johnny's complete inability to function. Despite holding playable cards throughout, it spent the critical early turns passing priority, then made a string of illegal or nonsensical plays once it finally acted. The 10 failed tool calls from Llama4 (mostly the mulligan loop) pale beside MiniMx Johnny's fundamental failure to engage with the game at all.

Llama4 Maverick played a competent if imperfect game — its zombie package came together naturally and it pressed its advantage. MiniMax M2.5, even at medium reasoning effort, looked completely lost from start to finish.
